Built-In Strategies

BATTLE-TESTED
ALGORITHMS

Each strategy can be backtested against 5,000 data points before going live.

🔴
RSI Divergence
Identifies overbought and oversold reversals using a configurable RSI period. Fires when RSI crosses back through the 70/30 threshold.
MOMENTUM
📑
Bollinger Breakout
Trades price breakouts above or below the Bollinger Bands. Configurable period and standard deviation multiplier.
BREAKOUT

MACD Crossover
Uses fast/slow EMA divergence and signal-line crossovers to detect trend changes. The gold standard for Deriv indices.
