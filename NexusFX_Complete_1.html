Built-In Strategies

BATTLE-TESTED
ALGORITHMS

Each strategy can be backtested against 5,000 data points before going live. Know your edge before you risk a cent.

🔴
RSI Divergence
Identifies overbought and oversold reversals using a configurable RSI period. Fires when RSI crosses back through the 70/30 threshold — a classic mean-reversion edge.
MOMENTUM
📑
Bollinger Breakout
Trades price breakouts above or below the Bollinger Bands. Configurable period and standard deviation multiplier. Effective in trending volatile conditions.
BREAKOUT
📈
MACD Crossover
Uses fast/slow EMA divergence and signal-line crossovers to detect trend changes. The gold standard momentum indicator tuned for Deriv volatility indices.
TREND
🌿
EMA Ribbon
Three-EMA ribbon crossover system (fast, slow, filter). The filter EMA acts as a regime detector — only trades in the direction of the trend, reducing false signals.
MULTI-EMA
